# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is organisations and decision-makers assessing a construction, fit-out, E&M, or maintenance partner across Macau, Hong Kong, and Zhuhai. They need to understand JWC's capabilities, inspect relevant project examples, recognize evidence of long-term client relationships, and reach the company directly. Existing clients must also be able to find contact information without friction.

## Product Purpose

This website presents Joint Win Construction & Engineering Company Limited as an experienced, accountable delivery partner. It connects the company's service capabilities to clear case evidence, shows how work progresses from brief to handover, demonstrates repeat-client relationships, and provides a direct route to company contact details. Success means a visitor can quickly identify a relevant service, inspect a matching case, and confidently continue to Cases or Contact.

## Positioning

JWC combines integrated construction and engineering capability with direct communication, active oversight, and accountable delivery for demanding commercial and operational environments.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary action: Explore our cases.
- Secondary action: Contact JWC using the dedicated Contact route.
- The line a visitor should remember after 10 seconds: “Build on trust. Build around your needs.”
- Belief ladder: JWC has operated since 2006; it can coordinate fit-out, E&M, and maintenance work; it has relevant case experience across sectors; clients return for additional work; the company remains directly reachable and accountable.
- Proof on hand: eight replaceable demo case records with raster imagery, the supplied service and delivery records, working statistics, selected-client names, and six literal repeat-client periods. These are demo or working records, not independently verified production claims. Official JWC logo masters, client logos, final approved photography, and bilingual translations have not been supplied.

## Brand Personality

Precise, accountable, and assured. The experience should create confidence and trust through measured hierarchy, architectural structure, real project imagery, and direct language. It must feel premium and corporate without becoming cold, generic, or theatrical.

## Anti-references

The website must not resemble a generic software landing page, playful technology template, or card-heavy dashboard. Avoid rounded-card grids, glossy gradients, pill-heavy controls, decorative glassmorphism, large shadows, continuous marquees, automatic carousels, bounce-heavy motion, aggressive zoom, and scroll hijacking. The locked Repeat Clients section must not gain timelines, calculated durations, hover cards, controls, filters, modals, or extra metadata. The locked Contact section must not gain a form or hide the General Manager placeholders.

## Design Principles

1. Treat the supplied original demo images as the visual authority and reproduce their proportions, hierarchy, crop, and geometry before adding interpretation.
2. Show evidence through cases, process, statistics, and repeat relationships; never invent permanent claims or present demo records as confirmed production facts.
3. Keep every content record semantic and replaceable so future editors can update imagery, names, periods, descriptions, and manager details without redesigning components.
4. Use motion as construction logic: build, measure, focus, and progress. Every transition must clarify hierarchy or state.
5. Keep contact and navigation direct. Required content remains visible, keyboard-operable, and readable at every supported width.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal conformance level is stated in the handoff. The implementation must nevertheless provide semantic landmarks and heading order, useful alternative text, fully keyboard-operable navigation, service tabs and filters, visible focus, sufficient contrast, stable content during transitions, reduced-motion support, responsive reading order, and no horizontal overflow. Language labels EN, 繁, and 简 must retain stable width; full translated content is not yet supplied.
